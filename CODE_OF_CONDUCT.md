# KubeBrowse Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want KubeBrowse to be a welcoming place to learn, build, and contribute. Everyone participating in the project must treat others with respect, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions, reviews, and other project interactions.
- Give constructive feedback about the work and welcome questions and different perspectives.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help resolve disagreements calmly.
- Help newcomers participate and make collaboration accessible.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or stalking.
- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in its review.
- Other conduct that undermines a safe and respectful community.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, community channels, events, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents KubeBrowse publicly or when behavior outside those spaces materially affects participation in the project.

## Reporting concerns

Sai Sanjay ([@sanjay7178](https://github.com/sanjay7178)), the lead maintainer, is responsible for receiving and reviewing reports.

Report concerns privately to **[sanjay@nullvijayawada.org](mailto:sanjay@nullvijayawada.org)**.

Reports should describe what happened, when and where it happened, and any relevant links or evidence. Include how you would like to be contacted and any immediate concerns. Share only information needed to review the incident. Do not post sensitive reports or personal information in public issues.

Reports will be handled as privately as possible. Information will be shared only when needed to investigate, respond, or meet legal obligations. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Reporters and witnesses must not face retaliation.

If a report concerns the person receiving it, request an independent reviewer through a project contact you trust. A reviewer involved in the incident must not decide its outcome. If an independent reviewer is unavailable, that limitation should be explained to the reporter.

## Review and enforcement

The maintainer will acknowledge reports as soon as reasonably possible, review the available information fairly, and communicate next steps to the reporter when possible. People involved may be asked for clarification. Responses will consider the severity, impact, and any pattern of behavior.

Possible responses include:

1. A request to stop or correct the behavior.
2. A warning and conditions for continued participation.
3. Removal of inappropriate content or a temporary restriction on participation.
4. A permanent ban from project spaces for serious or repeated misconduct.

Restrictions may be applied immediately when needed to protect participants. Enforcement decisions should be recorded privately with their rationale. Anyone affected may request reconsideration and provide additional information; an independent reviewer should handle that request where available.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers must model respectful behavior, explain community expectations, respond to reports, and apply this policy consistently. They may remove contributions or restrict access when conduct violates this policy. The reporting contact and reviewer arrangements must remain current as the project grows.
